3.4 Diagnostic Enzyme Markers

schedule 22 min Module 03: Lab Safety & Protocols

In this lesson, we explore the critical role of diagnostic enzyme markers in clinical settings. You will learn to identify key enzymes such as AST, ALT, and ALP, understand their normal physiological functions, and analyze what elevated or decreased levels indicate regarding tissue damage or disease states. We will focus specifically on interpreting these markers within the context of hepatic and cardiac profiles.
